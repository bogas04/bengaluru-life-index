What does life look like
for you right now?

A few small choices will shape your starting index. There’s no right answer, and you can change everything later.

What feels comfortable
each month?

Your total household spending budget, including rent and everyday expenses. Leave it blank to leave living costs out of your index.

To start, we estimate everyday expenses at 30% of your budget and use nearby rent samples. You can adjust that estimate later. Wards without rent coverage won’t be ranked when costs are included.

How would you like
to get around?

We’ll emphasize nearby services or transport stops. Journey times and footpath quality aren’t measured yet.

What would make
your days better?

Choose any that matter. We’ll give these factors more weight in your index.

Your answers create an editable preference score, saved in this browser. You choose whether to share it later. Creating this index replaces your current weights.
